How every lesson works

What — see the idea

Each concept is shown the way it appears in real records: statements, accounts and entries.

Why — follow the money

Every figure exists to help investors and creditors predict future cash flows. You'll learn the reason behind each rule.

How — do the work

You post the journal entries yourself and watch the ledger and the equation update at once.
