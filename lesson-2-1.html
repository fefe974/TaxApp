      Just a custom. Debits on the left and credits on the right is a convention — like driving on the right-hand side of the road. But the custom is now the rule, and it applies to all accounts.

      The double-entry system

      Dual effect

      Every transaction touches at least two accounts: each entry has a two-sided effect, recorded with equal debits and credits.

      Built-in proof

      If every transaction keeps debits equal to credits, then the sum of all debits must equal the sum of all credits — an automatic accuracy check.
