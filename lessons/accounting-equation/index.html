The Accounting Equation

In a double-entry system, for every debit there must be a credit, and vice versa. This leads us to the basic equation in accounting — the foundation on which every transaction is recorded.

Every time a transaction occurs, the elements of the equation change; the equality always remains. Like the basic equation, the expanded equation must always balance — total debits equal total credits.
