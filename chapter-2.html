Reversing Entries

Companies use reversing entries to simplify the recording of adjusting entries in the next accounting period.

December 31
Record the adjusting entry
DrSupplies Expense20,000
CrPrepaid Supplies20,000
Reverse next period
January 1
Reverse the entry
DrPrepaid Supplies20,000
CrSupplies Expense20,000

Key point

Reversing entries do not affect the financial statements. They only make the next period's adjusting entries easier to record.
